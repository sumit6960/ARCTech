Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

CADWorks ("we", "us" or "our") operates this website and provides CAD drafting and outsourcing services. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Contact details — your name, company name, email address and phone number submitted via the contact or quote form.
  • Project information — descriptions, deadlines, drawing requirements and any files you upload when requesting a quote.
  • Communications — emails, messages or other correspondence you send us.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic server logs including IP address, browser type, pages visited and referring URL. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics by default.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide a project quote.
  • To deliver the drafting services you have engaged us for.
  • To communicate project updates, questions and delivery notifications.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. File Handling

Project files you share with us (drawings, PDFs, field data, etc.) are used solely for the purpose of completing your drafting project. Files are not disclosed to third parties without your permission and are deleted or returned upon project completion unless you request otherwise.

4. Form Submission & Third-Party Processors

Our contact form may be processed via Formspree (formspree.io) or a similar third-party form service. By submitting the form, your data passes through their servers. Please review the applicable processor's own privacy policy for details on their data handling practices.

5. Data Retention

We retain contact and project information for as long as is necessary to fulfil the services requested and to meet our legal and accounting obligations. You may request deletion of your data at any time by emailing us.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction you may have the right to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at hello@cadworks.com.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss or disclosure. No method of electronic transmission is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
