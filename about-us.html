Our story

Built from the ground up for AEC teams.

CADWorks grew out of a straightforward problem: AEC firms needed dependable drafting help that understood their workflows and standards. Here's how we got here.

2016

Founded as a survey drafting studio

Started with a focus on land survey CAD — converting field data and notes into clean, client-ready drawings for small survey firms across the US.

2018

Expanded into civil and architectural

Added civil CAD and architectural drafting services as engineering and architecture clients came on board with similar needs for reliable production capacity.

2021

Introduced the 5-step QA workflow

Formalised our internal quality process — scope review, drafting, self-check, senior review, delivery — after client feedback confirmed that consistency mattered most.

2024

500+ projects completed across AEC

Passed 500 completed projects spanning survey, civil, architecture and developer clients, with a growing base of repeat customers relying on us as a long-term drafting partner.
