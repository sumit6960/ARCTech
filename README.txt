CADWorks website starter
========================
Open home.html to preview the site.

Before launch:
1. Replace "CADWorks" with the real company name.
2. Update email, phone and location.
3. Replace sample portfolio panels with real approved drawings/images.
4. Connect contact.html to Formspree, HubSpot, your CRM, or your own backend so enquiries and files are actually received.
5. Add your logo/favicon and real SEO title/description.
6. Add privacy policy and terms pages if required.
